## 3. Resolution of the Six Readiness Discrepancies
1. **TUM Path Length Calculation**: Subsampling MoCap at 1.0 s reproduces published benchmark metrics (XYZ: 5.782 m vs raw 6.618 m; Halfsphere: 7.519 m vs raw 8.536 m; Static: 0.284 m vs raw 0.630 m), eliminating high-frequency sensor noise summation.
2. **TUM Sequence Duration**: Confirmed physical timestamp span across all frames matching official dataset manifests.
3. **OpenLORIS Missing Frames**: Exactly 1,500 color and aligned depth images exist on disk (indices 0..1499). The remaining 389 entries in `color.txt` were unextracted rosbag indices; exactly 1,500 frames are matched and tracked.
4. **RealSense D455 Timestamp Skew**: Max skew across 4,981 pairs in session 1 is 21.75 ms, and across 5,283 pairs in session 2 is 22.14 ms. Zero violations of the 30 ms limit.
5. **OpenLORIS Extrinsics**: Ground truth trajectory is tracked in `base_link`, transformed to `d400_color_optical_frame` via calibrated rigid offset in `trans_matrix.yaml`.
6. **RealSense D455 Ground Truth Nature**: The raw file `groundtruth.txt` is an explicit identity placeholder (`tx=0, ty=0, tz=0, qw=1`). Camera trajectory ATE is strictly documented as `null`, avoiding false claims of trajectory accuracy.

## 12. Preservation of Negative Findings
- **Landmark-Only Downweighting Degradation (I2)**: Directly testing native ORB-SLAM3 landmark weighting confirmed that downweighting landmarks in local bundle adjustment without suppressing front-end feature detection causes tracking jitter (+0.0269 m degradation).
- **Static Feature Suppression Penalty**: Masking static objects (monitors, chairs, desks) in TUM XYZ degrades ATE by -0.0150 m to -0.0867 m due to visual anchor starvation, demonstrating that indiscriminate querying/masking is harmful.
- **Corridor Feature Starvation (OpenLORIS)**: In texture-poor corridors, aggressive person masking removes valuable visual anchors and slightly increases tracking drift (-0.065 m), underscoring the necessity of estimator-aware uncertainty thresholds.

## 13. Failure Modes, Edge Cases & Limitations
1. **Single-Sequence Overfitting**: Models trained on single sequences fail to generalize across differing camera optics and room dynamics, justifying the LOGO grouped validation protocol.
2. **Parallax vs Motion Ambiguity**: Fast camera rotation in halfsphere sequences induces high 2D optical flow on static objects; 3D covariance tracking in the estimator is required to prevent false dynamic alarms.
3. **Handheld Drift Accumulation**: Unbounded drift in handheld D455 sequences prevents camera ATE benchmarking; scene graph evaluation on static objects is the only valid metric.
